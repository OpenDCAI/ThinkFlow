# Paper Note Schema

Use only for comprehensive reading notes. Remove sections that the available evidence cannot support.

```markdown
# {Paper title}

## TL;DR
One sentence covering what the paper does and why it matters.

## Problem and Motivation
- Broader context
- Specific problem
- Why the problem is difficult
- Prior approaches and shortcomings
- Gap addressed by this paper

## Core Insight
The minimal idea that makes the approach work.

## Method Pipeline
An ordered explanation from inputs through training and inference to outputs.

## Formula Walkthrough
Only central equations. Define variables, explain the operation, and state why it is needed.

## Experiments
- Setup, datasets, baselines, and metrics
- Main quantitative results
- Ablations and controls
- Evidence that does or does not support each central claim

## Strengths
Specific strengths grounded in the supplied paper.

## Limitations
Author-acknowledged limitations plus evidence-backed additional limitations.

## Connections
Related methods, competing approaches, or concepts already discussed in this research space.

## Key Takeaways
Three to seven durable points.

## Open Questions
Unanswered questions and the experiments needed to resolve them.

## Research Ideas
Concrete extensions, combinations, or falsifiable hypotheses inspired by the paper.
```

For skim depth, use only TL;DR, Problem and Motivation, Core Insight, headline Experiments, and Key Takeaways.
