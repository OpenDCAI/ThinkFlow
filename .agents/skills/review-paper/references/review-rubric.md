# Review Rubric

Rate each relevant criterion from 1 to 5 and justify the rating with evidence. Omit criteria that do not apply.

| Criterion | Questions |
|---|---|
| Soundness | Are the argument, mathematics, implementation assumptions, and conclusions internally consistent? |
| Novelty | What is genuinely new relative to the closest prior work? Is the difference substantive? |
| Significance | Would the result change scientific understanding or practice for the intended audience? |
| Experimental design | Are datasets, baselines, metrics, controls, and budgets appropriate and fairly compared? |
| Statistical rigor | Are variance, uncertainty, repeated runs, significance, and multiple comparisons handled? |
| Ablations | Do ablations isolate the contribution of each important design choice? |
| Reproducibility | Are algorithms, hyperparameters, data processing, compute, and evaluation details sufficient? |
| Generalizability | Do the evidence and assumptions support the claimed scope? |
| Scalability | Are time, memory, compute, and deployment costs measured or convincingly analyzed? |
| Ethics and impact | Are data provenance, bias, privacy, dual-use, and affected groups handled appropriately? |

Paper-type emphasis:

- **Empirical**: controls, baselines, leakage, uncertainty, ablations, and generalization.
- **Theoretical**: definitions, assumptions, proof correctness, tightness, and relevance of the setting.
- **Systems**: architecture decisions, end-to-end measurements, bottlenecks, cost, and operational realism.
- **Survey**: coverage, taxonomy quality, selection methodology, synthesis, and recency.
- **Position**: argument coherence, evidence quality, counterarguments, and falsifiability.
